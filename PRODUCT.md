# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, in sequence:

1. **Recruiters / HR (first pass):** usually arrive on a phone via QR code or LinkedIn. They scan quickly to decide whether the candidate is worth an interview.
2. **Technical leads (second pass):** evaluate depth — architecture, production systems, and how problems were solved — before hiring.

The page must satisfy the fast scan without blocking the deep read.

## Product Purpose

Interactive portfolio and CV for Juan Manuel Puccio, Full Stack Developer & Process Optimization Specialist. Success means the visitor **contacts Juan** (email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp) to start an interview conversation.

## Positioning

Formed in robotics and programming, tested in real operations (ANSES, healthcare, logistics, food commerce). Builds and stabilizes production software used by real, paying customers — not demo projects.

## Operating Context

- Primary entry: mobile browser opened from a QR code; must feel instant.
- Secondary entry: desktop, via LinkedIn or direct link.
- Trilingual: Spanish (default), English, Portuguese.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 7 static site, TypeScript strict, deployed on Vercel.
- Islands architecture: JS only for widgets that need state.
- Zero CLS; mobile-first is mandatory.
- All copy lives in structured i18n sources, never hard-coded.

## Brand Commitments

- Taekwon-Do ITF belt progression (10th gup → 1st dan) is the narrative metaphor for technical growth.
- Name: Juan Manuel Puccio. Live at juanpuccio.vercel.app.

## Evidence on Hand

- Production systems: NodoSur, NodoFit, Satori Dojo, Don Pizza; also Stoky.
- Martial-arts trajectory and ITF principles content in the site.
- No testimonials, client logos, or metrics are confirmed — do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Contact is the destination; every section should make reaching out easier, not harder.
2. Scan first, depth second: a recruiter gets the verdict in seconds, a tech lead can keep digging.
3. Proof over claims: show real systems in production rather than adjectives.
4. Instant on mobile: the QR-scan moment is the first impression.
